WHO WE ARE

A studio built around three convictions

We don't ship features for the sake of volume. Every line of code, every pixel, every animation is judged against a single question — does it serve the user without ever asking them to compromise their data, attention or trust?

Minimalist by Default

We treat every interface as a Zen garden — every component earns its place, every interaction is intentional, and the absence of clutter becomes a feature in itself. Complexity is hidden; clarity is exposed.

Design Systems Reduced Cognitive Load Elegant Restraint

Privacy as Architecture

Privacy is not a toggle at the end of a settings menu — it is a load-bearing wall of our architecture. Local-first storage, on-device processing, encryption at rest, zero unnecessary telemetry. The data never leaves the device unless it absolutely must.

Local-First AES-256 Zero Telemetry

Experience-Obsessed

A technically correct product that feels clumsy is a failed product. We obsess over micro-interactions, motion curves, sound design and the silent theatre between tap and response — because delight lives in the details.

Micro-Interactions Motion Design Sensory Polish
CORE CAPABILITIES

Where programmatic generation meets immersive design

We don't rely on fixed templates or pre-baked assets. Our engineers compose live, on-device systems that breathe with the user — and our designers shape the surface to feel inevitable.

01

Programmatic Audio Synthesis

We build real-time audio engines that generate scores, ambient beds and rhythmic patterns on the fly. No audio files, no storage bloat — just math translated into sound, tailored to the listener's context and emotional state.

  • ▸ Realtime DSP pipelines
  • ▸ Procedural rhythm & harmony engines
  • ▸ Adaptive ambient synthesis
  • ▸ Offline-first score archiving
02

Immersive Visual Interaction

Our interface layer reads gestures, focus, ambient light and motion context to render scenes that feel alive. Animations are never gratuitous — they are a continuous dialogue between the user and the surface.

  • ▸ Procedural scene composition
  • ▸ Physics-aware motion
  • ▸ Contextual micro-animations
  • ▸ Haptic-synced transitions
03

Local Data Architecture

Our entire portfolio is engineered around a "data stays on the device" doctrine. We build our own encrypted local stores, our own sync layers (where users explicitly opt-in), and our own audit trails.

  • ▸ AES-256 encrypted local stores
  • ▸ Zero-knowledge cloud sync (opt-in)
  • ▸ On-device ML inference
  • ▸ User-controlled export & purge
04

Programmatic Visual Generation

We compose layouts, illustrations and even onboarding flows procedurally. The result is software that feels hand-crafted for every session, while remaining small, deterministic and testable.

  • ▸ Generative UI layouts
  • ▸ Procedural illustration
  • ▸ Adaptive typography systems
  • ▸ Deterministic animations

OUR PROCESS

From concept to App Store, end-to-end

Every project we accept goes through the same four-phase pipeline. No shortcuts, no black boxes, no surprises at submission.

01

Discovery

Domain modelling, persona mapping, privacy-impact assessment, regulatory mapping and architecture sketches.

02

Prototype

Interactive Figma prototypes, motion language experiments, on-device DSP/ML feasibility spikes and security review.

03

Engineer

Swift / SwiftUI / Metal builds, ATT/GDPR instrumentation, MMP wiring (AppsFlyer / Adjust / Singular), automated UI tests.

04

Launch

App Store / Play Store submission, ASO, privacy labels, ATT prompt flow design, post-launch monitoring and quarterly reviews.
