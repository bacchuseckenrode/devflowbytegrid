TEAM ADVANTAGES

Six reasons we consistently over-deliver

A studio is only as good as the constraints it sets itself. Here are the principles we hold the line on — and the practical edge they give every project we touch.

Home/Advantages
01

Veteran-only Engineering

Every member of our engineering bench has shipped consumer software at scale. Our average team tenure in iOS is over nine years, with deep specialisation across DSP, computer graphics, on-device ML and cryptography. We don't staff junior engineers on critical paths — and we don't subcontract your IP.

9+ yrs avg iOS DSP · CG · ML · Crypto No Outsourcing
02

Programmatic Generation Mindset

We treat fixed assets as technical debt. Layouts, illustrations, audio textures and even onboarding flows are composed at runtime from procedural primitives. The result is software that feels hand-made for every session while remaining small, deterministic and infinitely varied.

Procedural Audio Generative UI Procedural Illustration
03

Privacy as Load-bearing Wall

Privacy is not a settings toggle — it is a load-bearing wall of our architecture. Local-first storage, on-device processing, encryption at rest, opt-in sync, granular consent, no dark patterns. We've shipped this approach across six published apps and learned its sharp edges the hard way.

Local-First AES-256 Opt-In Sync No Dark Patterns
04

Cultural Sensitivity by Default

Our audience spans European, North American and emerging markets. We design for colour-vision accessibility, multi-script typography, low-bandwidth environments, multi-generational users, and the realities of shared household devices. Craftsmanship without inclusion is decoration.

WCAG 2.2 AA Multi-script Low-bandwidth
05

Compliance as Engineering Discipline

GDPR, UK-GDPR, CCPA, CPRA, VCDPA, LGPD, PIPEDA, DPDP, APPI, NDPA, PIPL, DSA, ATT — these are not paperwork, they are engineering requirements. We design consent flows, data residency and audit logs into the first sprint, not the last week before launch.

11+ Jurisdictions DSA · ATT · GDPR Audit-ready
06

Quiet, Long-term Partnerships

We don't scale our roster of clients — we scale the depth of attention we give them. Most of our engagements span multiple years, with quarterly reviews and roadmaps that evolve with the user's reality. We are not the cheapest bid; we are the last studio you'll need to hire.

Multi-year Avg. Quarterly Reviews Deep Attention
